Before posting

House rules for the month

No opening date

Say "coming soon" or "follow for news". No month, year, prices or booking links until the owners announce the opening.

Check travel facts

Fares, bus times and routes change. Confirm the General Santos route with JM before the December 2 carousel goes up, and leave fares off the slides.

Respect Tboli culture

Use only photos where people agreed to be shown, credit weavers and ArJay by name, and run culture posts past ArJay first.
