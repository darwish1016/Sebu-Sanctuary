Welcome to Sebu Sanctuary

A quiet place to breathe, high above the valley.

Sebu Sanctuary is a private retreat at Lake Seloton, near Lake Sebu in South Cotabato. Wake up to mist lifting off the mountains, spend the afternoon at the edge of the infinity pool, and gather for a fresh, home-cooked dinner as the hills turn gold.

Whether you're coming from General Santos, Manila or the other side of the world, this is your home for a few slow, restful days, with good food, fresh air and genuine Filipino hospitality.

  • Exclusive, private stay with no strangers sharing the pool
  • Ideal for families, couples, barkada and wellness getaways
  • Close to Lake Sebu's Seven Falls and local markets

Explore Lake Sebu

Lakes, waterfalls & Tboli culture.

Sebu Sanctuary sits at Lake Seloton, near Lake Sebu in South Cotabato. It's a highland region on the island of Mindanao, known for its lakes, the Seven Falls and the rich heritage of the Tboli people.
