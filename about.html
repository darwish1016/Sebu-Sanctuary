Our Purpose

To leave Lake Sebu a better region than we found it.

Sebu Sanctuary is a private retreat at Lake Seloton, near Lake Sebu in South Cotabato. It was created by a small team who believe a stay should leave both you and the place you visit better off.

Good food. Restful surroundings. Meaningful connections. Your time to live well, naturally.

  • Honouring the traditions of the Tboli people
  • Protecting the natural environment
  • Healthy, farm-to-table living

Our Mission

To create an authentic and sustainable sanctuary in Lake Sebu where people, culture, and nature thrive together.

We honor the traditions of the Tboli people, protect the natural environment, create meaningful opportunities for the local community, celebrate diversity and provide exceptional hospitality through responsible development, healthy farm-to-table living, and genuine human connection.

Our Vision

Sebu Sanctuary to become a recognized model of sustainable tourism in the Philippines, demonstrating how cultural heritage, environmental stewardship, community partnership, combined with exceptional hospitality can create lasting value for both visitors and the people of Lake Sebu.

We aspire to leave a legacy that inspires others to travel with purpose, respect local traditions, and contribute to stronger, more resilient communities.

Built here, by people from here.

Sebu Sanctuary was built with local builders and local talent, using skills and materials from around Lake Sebu. Our bamboo furniture is handmade by makers nearby, and the garden is planted with native species.

We also built for the future: solar panels and a battery bank on the roof, and charging for the first electric car in Lake Sebu.

The story of the build, in photos, is coming soon.

We don't just say sustainable. Here's what we do.

Small, practical choices that look after Lake Sebu and the people who live here.

Energy

Solar power, stored for the night

Solar panels on our roof and a battery bank help power the sanctuary with clean energy from the Mindanao sun, day and night.

Transport

The first electric car in Lake Sebu

We built charging into the house, backed by our solar system, so we can get around without the fumes.

Water

Refill, don't rebuy

Hot and cold drinking water on tap in the kitchen, so you can refill your bottle instead of buying plastic.

Food & garden

Grown and bought locally

We plant native species around the sanctuary and cook with produce from local farms and the Lake Sebu market.

Meet the owners.

The heart behind Sebu Sanctuary.

Co-Owner · Health & Wellbeing Advocate

Kingsley Klau

A retired Naval Officer who has embraced a life of balance, health and wellbeing. Kingsley is passionate about whole-of-life nutrition, healthy living and sustainable choices. He completed a Permaculture Design Course in Margaret River, Western Australia, and lives by its ethics of earth care, people care and fair share. He's also a professional photographer and teacher with more than 25 years' experience.

“Live Well. Eat Well. Be Well. All of Life.”

Co-Owner · Healthcare Educator

Paul Grant

For more than four decades Paul has dedicated his life to helping people through emergency care, education and leadership, working as a paramedic, intensive care clinician, university academic and international collaborator across Australia and beyond. Sebu Sanctuary is the next chapter of that journey: a place to reconnect and recover from the demands of modern life, and a base for healthcare, education and community partnerships in the Philippines.

“Leave every person, every community and every place better than you found it.”

Paul Grant, Co-Owner
